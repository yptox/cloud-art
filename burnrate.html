BURNRATE

an endless feed that burns its own cost

every image you scroll past is destroyed in proportion to the energy its generation is estimated to have consumed. the fire cannot be reversed. the ledger does not reset.

COST MODEL — ESTIMATES, CITED

energy — Luccioni, Jernite & Strubell, “Power Hungry Processing: Watts Driving the Cost of AI Deployment?” (2023): ≈2.9 Wh mean per generated image across measured text-to-image models, scaled here by model class.
water — Li, Yang, Islam & Ren, “Making AI Less ‘Thirsty’” (2023): roughly 1.8–12 L of freshwater per kWh, combining on-site cooling and off-site generation, varying by site and season.
coordinates — published locations of hyperscale data-center sites.

providers do not disclose per-query figures. estimation is the point: these costs are real, and deliberately obscured.

phase-1 prototype: the imagery in this feed is synthesized in-shader, live, by your own GPU — the watts on this page are drawn from the device in your hand.
